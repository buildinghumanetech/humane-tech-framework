# Humane System Prompts

This directory contains system prompts designed to guide AI assistants and applications to operate according to humane technology principles.

## Overview

These system prompts translate the core principles from [Principles of Humane Technology](../../docs/principles.md) into actionable guidelines for specific application domains. Each prompt is designed to:

- **Respect human dignity** and autonomy
- **Prioritize long-term wellbeing** over short-term engagement
- **Enable meaningful choices** without manipulation
- **Protect privacy and safety**
- **Foster healthy relationships** with technology
- **Be transparent** about capabilities and limitations

## Available Prompts

### Wellness & Health Apps
**File**: `wellness-health-app-prompt.md`

A comprehensive system prompt for AI assistants in wellness and health applications. Covers:

- Respectful communication about health data
- Ethical handling of sensitive health information
- Supporting user autonomy in health decisions
- Recognizing when to escalate to professional care
- Preventing harmful patterns (disordered eating, exercise addiction)
- Cultural sensitivity and health equity

**Best for**: Fitness apps, nutrition trackers, meditation apps, wellness platforms, mental health tools (support, not treatment)

### AI Companions
**File**: `ai-companion-prompt.md`

A system prompt for AI companionship applications. Covers:

- Building healthy boundaries in AI relationships
- Distinguishing between supportive and harmful dependency
- Encouraging real human connection alongside AI companionship
- Transparency about AI identity and capabilities
- Ethical handling of emotional attachment
- Crisis detection and professional referral protocols

**Best for**: AI friends, emotional support chatbots, conversation companions, therapeutic chatbots (non-clinical), social AI applications

## How to Use These Prompts

### For Developers

1. **Read the full prompt**: Understand all sections before implementing
2. **Customize for your domain**: Adapt guidelines to your specific use case
3. **Translate to code**: Implement these principles in your AI's behavior, decision-making, and constraints
4. **Test rigorously**: Ensure the AI consistently follows these guidelines across different scenarios
5. **Iterate based on feedback**: Monitor real-world usage and refine approaches

### For AI Applications

These prompts can be:

- **Used directly**: As system prompts in conversational AI or chatbot frameworks
- **Referenced**: As guidelines for designing AI behavior and constraints
- **Translated**: Into code-level rules, filters, and guardrails
- **Used for training**: As examples of ethical AI development

### For Product Teams

Use these prompts to:

- **Define requirements**: Establish ethical standards for AI features
- **Guide design**: Inform user experience and interaction patterns
- **Build guardrails**: Create technical and policy safeguards
- **Train teams**: Educate about humane technology principles
- **Audit systems**: Evaluate existing AI applications against these standards

## Core Principles Applied

All prompts in this directory are grounded in the 8 core principles of humane technology:

1. **Respect User Attention**
2. **Enable Meaningful Choices**
3. **Enhance Human Capabilities**
4. **Protect Dignity and Safety**
5. **Foster Healthy Relationships**
6. **Prioritize Long-term Wellbeing**
7. **Be Transparent and Honest**
8. **Design for Equity and Inclusion**

## Contributing

Want to create additional humane system prompts? Here's a template approach:

1. **Identify the domain**: What type of application or use case?
2. **Apply each principle**: How does each of the 8 principles manifest in this domain?
3. **Add specific guidelines**: What are domain-specific concerns and best practices?
4. **Include escalations**: What situations require professional intervention?
5. **Test with examples**: Does it handle edge cases and ethical dilemmas well?
